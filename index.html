three problems, solved

What the problem was, what I did, and what changed.

my role · Chose the clips and edited them.

01 · problem

Gateway Specialty's first scripted video series, which introduced the company's product directors, was fully produced and then shelved. Leadership saw the finished product and realized there was no clear strategy behind it.

02 · solution

The team pivoted to a lower-risk idea: short clips from existing webinar recordings. I chose which moments to clip and edited each one in Descript, cleaning up the audio and applying consistent branding.

03 · outcome

Early audio-waveform clips averaged 6.1% engagement. Real video footage lifted that to 9.4%. Employee culture videos hit 16.4%. Content built on real people and candid moments wins, and it now shapes our plan for future video.

my role · Researched and proposed HubSpot AEO and the Instagram launch; own LinkedIn content strategy.

01 · problem

Gateway Specialty had minimal digital visibility, and buyer research was shifting toward AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) and away from traditional search. Most of the industry, including Gateway Specialty, hadn't caught up.

02 · solution

I connected a trend I noticed on LinkedIn to a shift in Gateway Specialty's own submission data, researched HubSpot's AEO product, and built the business case. I paired it with a second initiative, launching Gateway Specialty's first Instagram presence. I also took ownership of LinkedIn content strategy, documented a process for the team, and ran A/B tests on posting days and content types.

03 · outcome

Gateway Specialty purchased HubSpot AEO following my proposal, and its insights shaped our LinkedIn strategy and the Instagram launch on October 1. Followers grew from 2,261 to 2,965, a 31% increase under my ownership, after a slower growth rate before. I also pitched TikTok to our VP as a future channel. It's still in the idea phase.

my role · Came up with the idea and built it.

01 · problem

Clients requesting an insurance quote had to work through multi-page PDF postcards, materials that were clunky to read and act on quickly.

02 · solution

I came up with the idea of consolidating that content into a single-page HTML document clients could act on in one place. I taught myself GitHub to build it, used Claude to write the page, and readied it for client distribution.

03 · outcome

Every client we send it to now uses it. Clients and the internal team have both responded well to how much simpler requesting a quote has become.
